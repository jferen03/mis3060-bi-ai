Strategy 3:
  Prompt: What should a BI dashboard for a loan portfolio committee include, and why? I want a quick 150 word answer to this question.
    First 150: A loan portfolio committee dashboard should cover four areas: portfolio composition (balances by product, segment, geography, vintage), credit quality (delinquency buckets, NPL ratio, charge-offs, watchlist migration), concentration risk (top borrowers, industry/geographic concentration vs. limits), and performance/profitability (yield, net interest margin, loan loss reserve adequacy, ROA on the book).
                Why these: the committee needs to spot deterioration early (delinquency trends and migration analysis catch problems before charge-offs hit), manage risk appetite (concentration limits prevent overexposure to any single sector or borrower), and evaluate whether the portfolio is being priced and reserved appropriately relative to its risk. Trend lines (quarter-over-quarter, year-over-year) matter more than single snapshots, since committees govern trajectory, not point-in-time status. Filters by business line and officer support accountability discussions, and a covenant/exception tracker flags policy breaches needing action. Together these give the committee both an early-warning system and the data to support capital and reserve decisions.
