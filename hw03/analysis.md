# HW03 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

Most of the executive changes I found came before the nearest earnings announcement. Of the 32 events in `corporate_events_timeline.csv`, 20 came before, 9 came after, and 3 fell in the same week (all 3 a few days before). The event count overstates Walmart, which has 13 rows and several duplicates of the same person, so I also counted each 8-K filing once. By filing, 11 of 19 came before the nearest earnings release, 7 came after, and 1 fell in the same week: Walmart's CEO succession announcement, 6 days before its November 2025 earnings. The pattern varies by company. Walmart (3 of 4 filings before, the fourth in the same week), NVIDIA (3 of 5), JPMorgan (2 of 3) and Apple (2 of 3) lean toward before, while Microsoft leans after (3 of 4), though three of Microsoft's four filings have no identifiable event and may cover only compensation. The gaps are usually weeks rather than days (median 29 days), and with only 3–5 filings per company the data does not show that companies deliberately time executive announcements around earnings.
