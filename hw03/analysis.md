# HW03 Analysis: Executive Changes vs. Earnings Announcements

For the most part, the executive changes I found happened before earnings, not after. Walmart's duplicate rows inflate the raw event count (20 before, 9 after, 3 in the same week), so I also counted each 8-K filing once. That still came out 11 before, 7 after and 1 in the same week, and the same-week one was Walmart announcing its new CEO six days before its November 2025 earnings. Walmart, NVIDIA, JPMorgan and Apple all tended to announce changes before earnings, but Microsoft went the other way, with 3 of its 4 filings coming after (though my script couldn't find an actual event in three of them, so they might just be pay updates). With gaps of about a month (median 29 days) and only three to five filings per company, I wouldn't say companies are deliberately timing these announcements around earnings, but it's an interesting tendency.
